# Fusion Fit (v1.0)

Home exercise app by Fusion Labs: no gym, just a chair, a wall, water bottles and a backpack.

## Features
- **Military Fit 50+**: an 8-week boot camp for older men (Recruit → Soldier → Ranger) with strength days, ruck marches and circuits.
- **Burn & Lose** (weight loss, 6 weeks) and **Home Strength** (build muscle, 8 weeks).
- **Stroke Recovery at Home**: seated and supported routines (Gentle start, Standing strength, Arm & hand, Walking & stairs) and a 6-week plan, with a safety check, BE FAST warning signs and carer tips.
- **49 exercises**, each with an animated illustration, a body map highlighting the muscles worked, household equipment, steps, form tips, easier/harder versions and safety notes.
- **Workout player**: timers, rest countdown with beeps, screen stays on, an "I feel unwell" button with emergency numbers (10177 / 112).
- **Progress**: streak, weekly stats, weight chart, history, and a military-style **fitness test** (2-min push-ups, 30-s chair stands, plank, 1.6 km).
- Health check (PAR-Q style) during setup. Calorie estimates from MET values.
- "Created by Fusion Labs" on every tab.
